# DISCLAIMER

Somatriq is a personal wellness analytics tool. It is **not a medical device**
and provides **no medical diagnosis, treatment, or advice**.

Analytics describe deviations from your own historical baselines. Allowed
language: "Your resting heart rate is substantially above your recent
baseline." Forbidden language: "You have condition X." Persistent or
concerning changes may warrant professional evaluation (spec §125).

Everything Somatriq reports — scores, insights, correlations, predictions — is
either measured, calculated by versioned algorithms, statistical association,
or model output. Associations are not causation (spec §82). Health decisions
are yours; consult a qualified professional for medical questions.
